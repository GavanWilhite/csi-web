# 00 — Client Direction, As Stated

This records what the client asked for, in their own framing. It is **not** design
guidance — art direction is the design agent's call. Everything below is the client's
input, captured so it does not get lost in relay.

## The two deliverables

1. **A refreshed Cognitive Security Conference page.** Content spec and real copy in
   `03-CONFERENCE-PAGE-SPEC.md`.
2. **A design system page.**

## Audience, as described

International national-security people, and folks at the bleeding edge of
**neurosecurity**, **cognitive warfare**, and **cybersecurity**.

## The client's read on the current site

> "It's very noisy. It's very '90s. It's very academic trying to be cool. And none of
> these are great looks for this."

Factual backing for that read is in `01-CURRENT-STATE-AUDIT.md`.

## Direction given

- Bring it under a more modern look that speaks **modern defense language**.
- Words used for the target feel: **rectilinear**, **thin lines**, possibly **dotted
  areas**, potentially **lightly dithered gradients** — and if gradients are used,
  "keep it light."
- **Default to light mode.** Being able to go back and forth between light and dark is
  good, but light should be the default.
- Explicit hesitation about leaning too hard into cybersecurity visual language,
  "'cause that stuff gets very gaudy."

## Scope

- Capture a fair amount of the original information.
- **Streamlining is explicitly fine.**

## Open items to confirm with the client

- Early-bird pricing is stated as ending July 16; the capture date is later than that.
  Confirm current pricing before publishing.
- The footer reads ©2025 while the conference is 2026.
- Whether **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** (the current typeface) is to be retained — it
  is an intentional, licence-free accessibility choice and the design agent should know
  it is in play either way.
