**Practical Skills for Sustaining Performance, Recovery & Team Effectiveness**

Cyber professionals are trained to manage threats, incidents, and operational risk—but

rarely receive training on managing the human effects of sustained vigilance, alert overload,

interruptions, and high-pressure decision-making. This interactive workshop provides a practical introduction to the neurobiology of stress in cyber operations and teaches evidence-based recovery tools that can be integrated directly into daily workflows. 

  

Participants Will Learn:

• How chronic vigilance and cognitive load affect attention, decision-making, & communication

• The difference between acute stress responses and alert fatigue

• Little-known signs that stress may be impacting decision quality, focus, and recovery

• Evidence-based 2-minute recovery tools that fit naturally into cyber workflows

• Practical strategies for supporting teammates during high-demand periods

  

Participants Will Leave With:

• A toolkit of rapid, evidence-based recovery practices

• Practical techniques for managing cognitive load and alert fatigue

• Simple methods for improving focus, recovery, and readiness

• Actionable strategies to help sustain team performance under pressure

  

Format: Single 45-Minute Workshop

• Understanding Stress in Cyber Operations

• Alert Fatigue vs. Stress Spikes

• Applied Recovery Skills Lab

• Q&A

  

Intended Audience:

SOC Analysts | Incident Responders | Threat Hunters | Security Engineers | Security Leaders

Dr. Sara Rabinovitch, PhD is a Workplace Psychologist in Technology and Cybersecurity, Clinical Psychologist, and Behavior Scientist dedicated to advancing sustainable performance in high-stress industries. She is the Co-Founder and Chief Clinical Officer of CyberReset & Pacific Mindful Wellbeing @ Work, where she designs and leads science-based tools and micro-interventions that translate the neurobiology of stress recovery into practical, measurable, and engaging workplace solutions.

  

Her work focuses primarily on cybersecurity, technology, and STEM environments, where chronic stress, alert fatigue, and burnout often remain under-recognized operational risks. Dr. Rabinovitch develops nervous-system-informed skills and rapid stress-recovery activations that help professionals regulate physiological stress responses, strengthen cognitive performance, and build long-term resilience in environments requiring sustained vigilance.
