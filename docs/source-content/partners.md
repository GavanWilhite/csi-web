## Why Become a Partner?

Get early access to cutting-edge research, frameworks, and taxonomies that shape the cognitive threat landscape, helping your organization stay ahead of what's next.

#### Brand Leadership

Position your brand at the frontier of cognitive security — the essential next phase of cyber risk management — as AI-powered social engineering becomes a defining threat.

#### Talent Access

Priority pathways to recruit or upskill talent on cutting-edge capabilities, responding to the industry's 3.5 million unfilled security roles and exponentially skyrocketing demand.

#### Thought Leadership

Be recognized as a partner with a globally respected nonprofit, led by pioneers driving standards, research, advocacy, and workforce development across an expanding market.

#### Measurable Returns

Drive measurable impact, enjoy robust tax benefits as a 501(c)(3) supporter, and receive regular KPI analytics reporting for your board or governance needs if required.

#### Policy & Standards

Access exclusive executive briefings, collaborate with leaders from powerful institutions, and shape new standards and policies adopted by both industry and government.

#### Field Stewardship

Board of Directors and Guidelines Workgroup seats for organizations ready to take an active, mission-aligned role in building the field alongside us.

## CSI is Different

  * Non-profit, vendor-neutral, and mission-first.

  * Rooted in advancing the science of cognitive security, not profit margins

  * Built by a global community, not gatekept by insiders.

  * Focused on unifying the field under shared standards.
