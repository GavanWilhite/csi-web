# Meetings

#### Private, Practitioner-Focused Conversations on Cognitive Security

We host regular community meetings that bring together experts, practitioners, and mission-driven peers for deeper conversations about the threats shaping cognitive security today.

 

These include:

 

  * Post-presentation Ask-Me-Anything (AMA) sessions with field leaders

  * A confidential discussion and support space for professionals working in human risk, behavior change, and security awareness

 

To ensure safe, meaningful dialogue, these gatherings are highly curated and intentionally under the radar.

# Workshops & Events

#### Applied Learning & Innovation with Real-World Impact

Our events aren’t built around hype or ego—they’re built to create something actionable and meaningful.

​

CognectCon is our flagship event: a hands-on, solution-focused summit that brings together diverse voices to tackle complex cognitive security challenges. Each gathering focuses on a specific theme—from cybersecurity standards to cognitive red teaming—and ends with tangible outcomes that move the field forward.

 

We also host:

  * Partner-supported meetups around the world

  * Live workshops and cohort-based experiences

  * Event-aligned resources and companion toolkits

## Programs

#### Cognitive Security Foundations & Up-Skilling Training

We’re actively building a library of structured, interdisciplinary learning programs to meet the urgent training needs of our community and world at large.

 

Our programs will offer:

  * Actionable cognitive security training grounded in real-world scenarios

  * Modules designed for both technical and non-technical professionals

  * Insight drawn from psychology, cybersecurity, influence operations, design, and more

 

This is not about theory for theory’s sake. Our goal is to offer strategic knowledge built to protect and strengthen cognitive systems.
