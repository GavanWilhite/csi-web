**Raptor v. Raven, How’s Your Oversight?**

Tomm Larson will share the costs and benefits of running a cybersecurity awareness program for a Federal Contractor.

Tomm Larson currently serves as the Cyber Security Awareness Manager for the Idaho National Laboratory. He has been working in cybersecurity since 2002. He has worked in corporate, not-for-profit, and government organizations. He developed his passion for Cyber Security at a large antivirus vendor, and then honed his instruction skills while working at the Center for Infrastructure Assurance and Security. Just before joining Idaho National Laboratory, he served as the Cyber Security Awareness Manager at Las Vegas Sands Corporation.  He holds a B.A. in Communications from Brigham Young University and an M.B.A from the University of Texas at San Antonio.
