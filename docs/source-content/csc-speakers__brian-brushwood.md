**The Solution to the Scampocalypse Is More Scams. (Seriously)**

Traditional awareness training teaches people what to look for. Cognitive defense requires teaching them how adversaries think. In this highly interactive workshop, Brian Brushwood demonstrates Wolfmasking—a supervised deceptive role-play methodology that transforms your highest-risk "always-clicking" employees into people who can think like social engineers, without becoming them.

  

Built around the deceptively simple R.E.A.D. teaching method and validated in a Clemson University pilot now expanding to additional cohorts, attendees won't just hear the theory—they'll experience the same intervention themselves and leave with a practical framework for building genuine cognitive resilience in hours, not weeks.

Brian Brushwood is an American magician, author, podcaster, and social engineering educator who has spent 25 years making a career out of understanding — and demonstrating — exactly how human beings are fooled. He performed 100–200 live shows per year on college campuses for two decades, combining fire-eating, mind reading, and dangerous stunts with a deeply practical education in deception and cognitive vulnerability. He is the creator of _Scam Nation_ , the self-described "only show dedicated to social engineering at the bar and on the street," which has accumulated over 1.8 million YouTube subscribers. He co-created _The Modern Rogue_ and hosts _World's Greatest Con_ , a critically acclaimed podcast deconstructing history's greatest deceptions — from street hustles to Operation Mincemeat. He has appeared twice on _The Tonight Show with Jay Leno_ , on _Anderson Cooper 360°_ , and starred in _Hacking the System_ on the National Geographic Channel. He holds a Plan II honors degree from the University of Texas at Austin and before becoming a full-time performer, designed high-end computer systems at Dell.
