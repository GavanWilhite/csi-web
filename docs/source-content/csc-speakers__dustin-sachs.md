**What Five Minutes Under Pressure Teach Us About Decision Ecosystems**

Five minutes under pressure can reveal more about an organization than hours of normal operation. As time contracts and consequences rise, people rely less on deliberate reasoning and more on the conditions surrounding the decision. What appears to be individual success or failure is often the visible outcome of an entire decision ecosystem. The lesson for leaders is practical. Resilience cannot be built through motivation, awareness, or training alone. Organizations must examine how their culture, workflows, technology, governance, and performance expectations interact when pressure is greatest. Decision Ecosystem Optimization begins there: redesigning the conditions around consequential decisions, so the safer action becomes easier, more likely, and, wherever possible, the default.

Dr. Dustin S. Sachs is a cybersecurity executive and behavioral scientist who helps organizations convert security strategy into everyday secure behavior. He is the Founder & CEO of PsyberCog Labs, LLC and serves as Chief Technologist & Senior Director of Programs at CyberRisk Collaborative. He focuses on decision quality, culture change, and friction-aware control design. An adjunct professor and frequent speaker, he publishes thought leadership and research on human factors, cognitive security, and decision ecosystem optimization. He is the author of the #1 Best-Selling Security Design book “Insights in Cybersecurity: A Guide to Digital Human Factors”, which provides tools, nudges, and decision frameworks to embed secure behavior at scale.
