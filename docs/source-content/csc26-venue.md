Planning Your Attendance

 

Attending CSC 2026 may require advance coordination with your organization. To support your travel request, we've prepared two ready-to-use HR travel justification briefs — written for different professional contexts but equally useful for making the case internally.

​

#### Travel Brief: Human Risk & Security Professionals

For those coming from cybersecurity, insider threat, behavioral risk, security awareness, or organizational security backgrounds.  
​

#### Travel Brief: Information Operations & Cognitive Warfare Professionals

For those coming from information operations, PSYOP, strategic communications, influence operations, cognitive warfare, or related national security fields.
