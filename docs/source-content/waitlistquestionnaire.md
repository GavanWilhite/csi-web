# CSI Course Waitlist Questionnaire

Let's get you placed in a course.

  

Answer 6 quick questions that will take about 3 minutes.

  

The more specific you are (especially on format and budget) the better your shot at getting a seat in a course built around what you actually need.

  

There's no "right" profile for a seat; we're looking for the clearest signal, not a particular answer.

  

Which best describes your current professional role?

Human risk management / security awareness professional

Researcher or academic (psychology, behavioral science, neuroscience, etc.)

Military / Government

Cybersecurity (IT security practitioner, GRC, etc.)

AI/ML security or safety professional

Security leadership (CISO, security program manager, etc.)

Student / early career professional

Other

What's the specific situation, project, or problem at work (or in your career) that made you sign up for this waitlist? (A sentence or two is plenty; we're trying to understand the real-world need behind your interest.)

If you could only choose one right now, which format would you be most likely to enroll in first?

A short workshop or webinar (1-3 hours, single session)

A multi-day intensive course (e.g., 2-3 full days)

A structured multi-week course (e.g., weekly sessions over 4-8 weeks)

A self-paced online course you complete on your own schedule

An ongoing certification or credentialing program (longer-term, recognized credential)

Other

If your employer were paying (e.g., as part of a training/professional development budget), what's the most you think they'd approve for a CSI course in this format?

Under $1,000

$1,000–$2,500

$2,500–$5,000

$5,000–$10,000

$10,000+

Not sure / no budget process at my organization

If you were paying out of your own pocket, what's the most you'd realistically spend?

Under $300

$300–$600

$600–$900

$900–$1,500

$1,500+

I would not pay for this myself, only if free or employer-funded.

I would enroll immediately / this is a current priority

Likely — I would plan to enroll within the launch window

Possible — depends on timing, budget approval, or workload
