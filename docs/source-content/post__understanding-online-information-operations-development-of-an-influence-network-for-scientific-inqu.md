# Understanding Online Information Operations: Development of an Influence Network for Scientific Inquiry Testing Environment (INSITE)

  * CSI
  * Jul 3, 2018
  * 1 min read

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/439552_4a763e23891b4368b9879325fe7a1096~mv2.avif/v1/fill/w_147,h_101,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,blur_2,enc_avif,quality_auto/439552_4a763e23891b4368b9879325fe7a1096~mv2.avif)

Influence operations that spread propaganda, disinformation, and social hysteria pose a critical threat to U.S. national security. Effective countermeasures must be developed to respond quickly and anticipate future threats. A major challenge in creating these countermeasures is the lack of a sophisticated testing environment for evaluating detection tools and algorithms. By integrating social sciences with applied mathematics, researchers can better understand social influence and response behaviors. The proposed solution involves creating a researcher-controlled social media platform to test and develop social engineering countermeasures in a realistic environment, providing crucial data and testing opportunities.

  

**_Authors:_**___Courtney Crooks, Tom McNeil, Ben Sawyer, Matthew Canham, David Muchlinski_

[ Read Paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/08995605.2018.1478546?journalCode=hmlp20)
